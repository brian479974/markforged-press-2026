Customer case · Subaru of Indiana Automotive (U.S.)

Many models on one line, and the right jig at a glance

Subaru of Indiana Automotive (SIA) runs several vehicle models on one assembly line, and their tooling can differ by just a few millimeters. A mix-up means rework and downtime, so SIA turned to color coding to make the right tool obvious.

Official Markforged film.

Rob Cooper of SIA explains that with three different models running, there is little time to work out which jig to pull, and you may already be behind; that one visual cue makes sure you grab the right jig.
Before: 3D-printed ABS3D-printed ABS provided color for low-volume custom parts, but not the durability automotive manufacturing requires. On a floor where tools are picked up, set down and reused hundreds of times a shift, SIA needed a stronger solution.
After: Onyx GFOnyx GF provided the strength and the color coding, running within SIA's existing Markforged ecosystem for seamless integration.

Bumper check jig

SIA tracks every bumper through the paint shop with hand-applied QR labels. Bumpers have no built-in locating features, so placement varied and fixed overhead scanners missed reads, stopping the line for manual tracking, re-scanning and reconciliation. SIA built a family of placement jigs that index off the bumper's outer geometry, putting each label within a 0.1 mm tolerance. Every model, front and rear, and every trim level has its own jig, and in Onyx GF each model variant has its own color.

Label placement within 0.1 mm

Assembly jig

A cradle jig for handling cam cap carriers needed a base component. Printed in ABS, it was prone to cracking. Printed in Onyx GF Yellow, it held up where ABS had failed, and its color marks where it belongs on the floor.

ABS cracked; Onyx GF Yellow held up
The resultOn a multi-model line, tool mix-ups mean rework and downtime. Onyx GF gave SIA the color differentiation to eliminate misidentification and the strength to survive repeated use: operators grab the right tool on the first pass, and the line stays moving.
