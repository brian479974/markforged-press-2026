Markforged Appoints TwoWay as Master Distributor for Vietnam
Markforged x TwoWay | Vietnam | 2026.10.05
Scope: sales, sub-distribution, demo centres, training and service across Vietnam
Planned demo centres: Hanoi · Ho Chi Minh City
TAIPEI, 5 October 2026 — Industrial additive manufacturing company Markforged has appointed TwoWay Group Co., Ltd. as its Master Distributor for Vietnam. TwoWay, a Markforged Diamond Partner already serving the Taiwan market, is authorised under a certificate issued by Markforged, Inc. to purchase, market, sell, support and service Markforged products throughout Vietnam, and to appoint and manage authorised sub-distributors in the country.

TwoWay plans to open Markforged demonstration and application centres in Hanoi in the north and Ho Chi Minh City in the south, where customers can see the systems running and validate their own applications.

Scope of appointment

Four areas set out in the certificate

The certificate is issued by Markforged, Inc. as an act of the company and sets out four areas of authority for TwoWay as Master Distributor:

Sales and servicePurchase, market, sell, support and service the Markforged product line throughout Vietnam
Sub-distributionAppoint, quote to and manage authorised sub-distributors and resellers in Vietnam
Demo centresEstablish and operate Markforged demonstration and application centres in Vietnam
Training and supportProvide training, technical support and after-sales service in Vietnam

The authorised products are the Markforged industrial 3D printing line — FX10, FX20, X7, X7 Field Edition and the FX10 with Metal Kit for metal parts — together with Markforged materials and Digital Forge software and services.

The certificate also names Brian Chen, Country Manager for Greater China and Vietnam, as the Markforged representative responsible for the territory; commercial matters under the appointment are managed through him.

Channel and demonstration centres

An authorised reseller network and two demonstration centres

Authorised resellers in Vietnam will receive quotations, supply and technical support from TwoWay, which is also responsible for building the authorised reseller network in the country and for demonstration, training and after-sales service.

The demonstration centres are planned for Hanoi in the north and Ho Chi Minh City in the south. The first demonstration systems are an FX20, an FX10 with Metal Kit (with a Wash-1 and a Sinter-2 furnace) and an X7 Field Edition, covering composite, metal and deployable applications.

Left: the FX10 industrial 3D printer. Right: the FX10 print bed fitted with the Metal Kit. Metal parts are finished by washing in a Wash-1 and sintering in a Sinter-2. (Images: Markforged)

Product line

The systems available in Vietnam

FX10 is Markforged's industrial composite 3D printer, using continuous fiber reinforcement to strengthen parts, with a build volume of 375 × 300 × 300 mm. Fitted with the Metal Kit, the same machine prints metal; parts shrink during sintering, and the maximum finished part size is 292 × 132 × 131 mm and 10 kg.

FX20 is Markforged's largest composite system, with a single-nozzle build volume of 525 × 400 × 400 mm. It prints high-temperature engineering polymers including ULTEM 9085, for large end-use parts that must withstand heat.

X7 is an industrial composite printer with in-process laser inspection of part dimensions. X7 Field Edition is the deployable version of the X7, packed in a rugged transport case and able to run on generator power for production away from the factory.

About Markforged

Markforged is an industrial additive manufacturing company founded in 2013. Through the Digital Forge platform and Continuous Fiber Reinforcement (CFR), manufacturers produce high-strength end-use parts, tooling and spares at the point of production, shortening supply chains, reducing inventory and improving capacity resilience. Its product line covers industrial composite and metal additive manufacturing systems, used in aerospace, automotive, electronics, medical devices and precision engineering.

About TwoWay

TwoWay Group Co., Ltd. was founded in 1992 and is headquartered in New Taipei City. It listed on the Taiwan Stock Exchange in 2024 (stock code 8045). The group runs two business units, broadband networks and AIoT, and has more than thirty years in cable television and communications, developing and manufacturing RF and optical transmission equipment. TwoWay is a Markforged Diamond Partner covering Taiwan and Vietnam.
